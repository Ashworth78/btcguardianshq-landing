Free, open-source tools to help your family inherit your bitcoin. No custodian, no KYC, no token. Your seed stays offline, split into encrypted shares only your heirs can put back together.

How it works

Four steps, all offline where it matters.

01

Create shares offline

Run vault.js on Tails with networking off. Your seed is split 3-of-5 with Shamir, and each share is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using an Argon2id key from your passphrase.

02

Store 5 shares apart

Keep the shares in different places or with different trusted people. One or two shares alone reveal nothing.

03

Heirs recover with 3

Any 3 shares plus the passphrase rebuild the seed, offline on Tails, following the plain-English heir runbook.

04

Restore to a hardware wallet

The recovered seed goes onto a new hardware wallet, and the funds are moved under the heir's own control.

Works now / Not yet

Honest about where it is.

Works now

  • 3-of-5 Shamir shares of a BIP39 seed
  • Argon2id + AES-256-GCM encryption
  • Fully offline use on Tails
  • Heir runbook in plain English
  • Own archival Bitcoin Core node (proofs)
  • Private, LAN-only Mempool explorer

Not yet

  • Multisig
  • Timelocks / on-chain rules
  • PSBT signing
  • Miniscript inheritance (being tested on testnet)
  • Independent review completed

vault.js is an unaudited prototype. Do not trust real funds to it yet. Test with fake data only.

On-chain tools such as Liana, Nunchuk and Keeper enforce inheritance rules in Bitcoin itself. BTCGuardiansHQ does not do that yet: it protects the seed off-chain. Each approach has trade-offs; pick what fits you.

Why it matters

A lot of bitcoin is lost because nobody planned for what happens next. Free, open, verifiable tools mean any family can plan an inheritance without handing their keys or identity to a company.
